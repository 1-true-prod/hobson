# The wizard's pictures

Four modules show a photograph; the other three (EVENTS, PRESENCE, COMMIT) are drawn by `art.js`
itself. Each photograph here is a public-domain work, cropped to 280 × 185, converted to
greyscale and contrast-stretched; `art.js` then dithers it to the module's colour as it plays.
Nothing cut from a film or a TV programme is shipped, however it is filtered: a treated frame is
still a copy of that frame.

Each status is as Wikimedia Commons records it on the linked page, checked 2026-09-27.

A new picture follows the same rule: a public-domain or openly licensed source, credited here, or
drawn in code. Add its file to `STATIC` in `scripts/setup_wizard.py`, or the wizard is refused it.
