# The wizard's pictures

Each module is the part of the brain that does its job, and its picture is an old anatomical
plate of that part: Vesalius (1543), Descartes (1664) and Gray's *Anatomy* (1918). Every plate
here is a public-domain work, cropped to 560 × 370, converted to greyscale, inverted (white lines
on black) and contrast-stretched; `vision.png` is also mirrored, and Descartes' plate number
painted out, so the head sits on the right, clear of the title. `art.js` rings and names the
region and dithers the frame to the module's colour as it plays. Nothing cut from a film or a TV
programme is shipped, however it is filtered: a treated frame is still a copy of that frame.

Each status is as Wikimedia Commons records it on the linked page, checked 2026-09-27.

A new picture follows the same rule: a public-domain or openly licensed source, credited here, or
drawn in code. Add its file to `STATIC` in `scripts/setup_wizard.py`, or the wizard is refused it.
