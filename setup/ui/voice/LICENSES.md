# The wizard's voice clips

Every `.m4a` here was synthesised by `scripts/cache-gen/setup_voice_gen.py`; `manifest.json` says
which clip is which. No recording of a real person's voice is shipped, and none may be: a voice
cloned from someone's recording is not ours to redistribute.

## Pocket TTS voices — CC BY 4.0

The narrator (Charles) and the Pocket TTS voices offered in the wizard (Charles, Paul, Anna, Vera,
Fantine, Eponine).

- **Model:** [Pocket TTS](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts) by Kyutai,
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Voices:** speakers p254 (Charles), p259 (Paul), p228 (Anna), p229 (Vera), p244 (Fantine) and
  p262 (Eponine) of the [CSTR VCTK Corpus](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443)
  (Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as enhanced and published by Kyutai
  in [kyutai/tts-voices](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices).
- **Changes:** new speech synthesised from those voices, peak-normalised and encoded as AAC.
